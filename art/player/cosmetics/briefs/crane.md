# Beast Folk (Crane), user's prompt (2026-10-23)

Create a stylized 3D cosmetic accessory set for a Roblox cultivation fantasy game for a Beast Folk (Crane) race.

Design only the accessories, not a full character.

Included cosmetics:
- A small red crown-patch crest placed on the top/front of the head, inspired by a crane's crown.
- White-and-black feather plumes flowing from the back of the head, elegant and slightly layered.
- A short tail fan: compact, refined, and feathered, with a fan-like silhouette rather than a mammalian tail.

Style direction:
- Graceful, spiritual, airy, and refined.
- The cosmetics should feel scholarly and elegant, like a sacred crane spirit from a cultivation sect.
- Prioritize clean feather silhouettes and noble posture over complexity.

Color direction:
- Main feathers: white.
- Accent feathers: black.
- Crown patch: muted but clear crimson red.

Modeling requirements:
- Low-poly, Roblox-friendly, and optimized.
- The crown crest, rear feather plumes, and tail fan should be separate mesh elements when useful.
- Use layered simplified feather shapes rather than many thin individual feathers.
- The back plumes should frame the head without excessive clipping.
- The tail fan should sit naturally at the lower back and remain readable from the rear.
