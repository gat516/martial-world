# Sect Breathing aura

Covers the Sect Breathing method aura (template `Effects.MethodAura.SectBreathing`): its brief, what is built, its budget and gaps; read it when changing this aura, after the shared rules in [auras](../systems/auras.md).

## Brief
- **Idea:** the basics
- **Mortal aura (the brief):** modest but deliberate (user, 2026-10-21): a small dantian glow that gathers motes on the inhale and settles on the exhale, a faint turning taiji under you, a short jade ignition
- **Higher grades:** — (the starter; it has no higher grade)
- **Refs:** — (see [auras](../systems/auras.md#reference-images))

## What's built (Mortal; reworked, modest on purpose: 6 loop emitters, no parts drawn)
- **On the body:** a small jade dantian glow with a hot centre, swelling with the breath; faint motes drawn in to it from about a body-width on the inhale; a faint jade qi vapour lifting off the head
- **Ground / around:** a faint ink taiji (black and white, its splattered dark half the dark base) turning slowly under you, a soft jade halo breathing round it
- **Breath beat:** the exhale settles: a glint at the dantian, a soft ring breathing out over the taiji, two or three motes rising off the belly
- **Ignition:** a jade flash and star at the dantian, motes thrown up that drift back down, a ring and a low puff of qi over the ground; the taiji and vapour surge

## Budget (R6)
6 loop emitters, about 12 live particles (motes 3/s on the inhale), one SurfaceGui (the taiji), no light, no parts drawn; its ignition adds ~22 particles for about a second.

## Known gaps
- Frost Lotus and Sect Breathing: previewed in Edit mode on an R6 rig (grass
  at noon and night) through the plain emitter fallback; play speed, R15,
  phones and the live sit / switch / breakthrough ignition are untested. The
  library has no lotus-petal texture, so the petals are a veined leaf image
  laid on flat parts in rings (they read as a glass lotus from the usual
  camera, edge-on from level). On a blocky R6 rig the seated arms hide most
  of what sits within ~2 studs of the body, which is why both ground figures
  reach about 3.5 studs out; slimmer avatars show more of them. Sect's taiji
  is a bold black-and-white image kept faint; its ignition surge brings it
  near full strength for a moment.
