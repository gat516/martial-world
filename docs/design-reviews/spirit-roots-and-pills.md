# Review: spirit roots, washing and rebirth pills

**Owner's decision (user, 2026-10-24):** pity fragments yes (bosses and treasures without a pill give Marrow Washing fragments, rarer Rebirth fragments; 10 fuse into a pill; the Pavilion sells them weekly-capped; never Robux). Earth and Heaven keep their names; a rare Immortal grade was added on top. The heaven-defying flag is shown as "Defier of Heaven". Rare roots got stronger perks plus a second perk and an element glow on their aura. Built: see [spirit root](../character/spirit-root.md).

**Verdict: keep with changes.** The reveal is excellent. It's free, it needs no decisions, there's a pillar per element, and it says "this cultivator is you" at minute 5. The pills are a long hunt with no pity and no gardener road, the rarest roots get the mildest perks, and "Heaven grade" collides with "heaven-defying". Reviewed 2026-10-23 from spirit-root.md and `Pacing.Roots`; nothing played.

## Why it exists / who it's for
- Arrow: a small perk on home or out, an element that matches techniques, and a long-term goal (grade). It serves kids (identity, spectacle, collecting) and teens (min-maxing, rare-root status). The element is shown to others through the `Root` attribute.
- Good: everyone starts at Spirit ("nobody is told their root is trash"). A reroll can always be undone. No Robux anywhere. That's an ethically clean variable reward.

## Problems
- **Rare roots underwhelm.** Lightning (4%) gets +2 walk speed, and Wind (3%) +15% jump. Fire and Metal (18%) get +8% damage. A kid who sees the tall rare pillar, the sky burst and the excited elder then gets the weakest practical perk. Teens will reroll *away* from rare roots, which inverts the status.
- **Pills need ~100-400 boss kills.** Earth takes 3 Marrow Washing Pills at 2-4% per boss kill (about 75-150 kills), and Heaven takes 8 (about 200-400). Bosses are optional, so the gardener's only source is a heavenly treasure at about 1%. Heaven grade is +25% cultivation speed, a real power gap that fighters reach and gardeners effectively never do, against "two complete roads". With no pity timer, one unlucky player can go 200 kills dry.
- **Per-element bookkeeping.** Separate grades and washing progress for each element, plus a pending choice. It's safe, but it's a lot of rules behind one tile. It's acceptable only because it stays hidden until a pill is held.
- **The name collides.** "Heaven grade root" (good, +25%) and "heaven-defying root" (1.5x bars, tribulations) will be confused by every player and in translation.
- **Wood's perk is "while online"**, another online-only rule (see afk-seclusion-offline).

## Metrics
The reveal is a strong first-session high and an honest thumbnail. Pills add a D30 goal for fighters only. Adding a fragment track adds a steady reason to fight bosses together, a co-play hook.

## Recommendations (ranked)
1. **A pity fragment.** Every boss kill without a pill drops a Marrow fragment, 10 fragments make a pill, and the Pavilion sells fragments for contribution (weekly-capped). That gives a bounded goal and a gardener road. Drop luck stays free play, so there are no paid-random concerns.
2. **Rare roots get a visible signature on top of the perk:** a permanent element trail or aura tint, and perks raised to match the commons in value (Lightning +3 speed and +4% damage, for example).
3. **Rename the grades** (Spirit, Earth, Sky or Celestial) so "heaven" means only heaven-defying, and the tribulation fiction stays clear.
4. Make Wood's growth perk apply offline too.
