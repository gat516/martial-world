# Mountain Breath Method aura

Covers the Mountain Breath Method (Earth) method aura (template `Effects.MethodAura.MountainBreath`): its brief, what is built, its budget and gaps; read it when changing this aura, after the shared rules in [auras](../systems/auras.md).

## Brief
- **Idea:** deep, steady as stone
- **Mortal aura (the brief):** an amber core low in the belly; a carved stone rune ring under you that settles a little with each breath; a few pebbles lifting very slowly; dust
- **Higher grades:** stones orbit in a heavy ring; Heaven: a mountain silhouette behind
- **Refs:** (none yet) (see [auras](../systems/auras.md#reference-images))

## What's built (Mortal; reworked: 18 loop emitters, no parts drawn)
- **On the body:** a low earth core in the belly (an amber crusted glow that swells with the breath, a hard dot, a level horizon glint, the one PointLight); a squat amber glow behind the lower body on a dark umber copy (a hill, not a flame); amber earth qi clinging to the torso and legs; gold sand sifting down off the arms and torso with dark grit flecks (the motion is downward: things settle)
- **Ground / around:** dry cracked earth (dark base) with amber veins glowing through it; a dark square-in-circle seal turning very slowly under a bold amber four-fold seal turning the other way (earth is square), both sinking a little at the breath's peak; stones and a few lit spirit stones orbiting slowly at 2.3 to 2.8 studs (emitters locked to spinning anchors), lifting on the inhale and settling on the exhale; gold dust hanging in the air; a low dust haze creeping out
- **Breath beat:** the settle on each exhale: a dust ring rolls out low, puffs of dust, a few pebbles hop, the core swells. Every 3 or 4 breaths (0.5 s jitter, 1.3x swell) a tremor: amber cracks flash across the ground, a bigger dust ring and spray, grit jumps, a flash and a wide level glint at the core; sand and grit surge
- **Ignition:** the ground thumps: a dark crater under amber cracks flashing out, rubble crumbling, a dust ring and a low dust spray, rocks thrown up, stone shards jutting up round the seat and sinking, a flash and a wide level glint at the belly, gold dust bursting up and raining back; sand and grit surge

## Known gaps
- Mountain Breath: previewed in Edit mode on an R6 rig (grass at noon and
  night) through the plain emitter fallback; play speed, R15, phones and the
  live sit / switch / breakthrough ignition are untested. On a blocky R6
  rig the squat back glow mostly hides behind the body; the ground and the
  orbiting stones carry it. Built without a reference.
