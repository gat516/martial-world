# Review: onboarding lessons after the hook (QC4 to FE)

**Owner's decision (2026-10-24):** approved with changes, built in [onboarding](../systems/onboarding.md) "One idea per chapter": QC4 Home only; Seclusion in the second session (45 s); QC5 the Borderlands + Rumours; QC7 stays the Dao drill (not Build); QC9 the heart demon; QR1 WeaponRack; building at QR2; the foundation line on the QC4->5 card; lines at 12/5 words; the wall card as free play's objective line; lessons instrumented (`Lesson` shown/claimed).

**Verdict: keep the system, rework the schedule.** One rewarded lesson, said once and guided, is the right tool. But the schedule:
- teaches a screen (Board) instead of the new region;
- leaves Explore, half of "out", until hour one;
- stacks two lessons at QC4 and two at QR1;
- goes silent for QR2-QR9, about 7 active hours;
- never teaches three live systems: the foundation grade, building and the heart demon.

After QC4 the objective line names a destination, not an action. Reviewed 2026-10-23 from `onboarding.md`, `Quests.luau`, `Pacing.luau`, `roadmap.md` §2; nothing played.

## The elder's voice
- Good: one warm, consistent master ("qi does not like to be rushed"). Story over rules, done well.
- Too long: 16-24 words over 2-3 sentences. WeaponRack is 126 characters against the doc's own limit of 90. Split the roles: the **elder line is why** (one sentence, at most 12 words), the **objective is what** (at most 5 words, icon and number), **Talk is the detail**. Noun stacks like "deeper weapon Dao" translate badly.

## The objective line and the wall card
In free play the line falls back to "Break through to Qi Condensation 5 (123/680)" (`Quests.goals`): where you're going, not what to do now. The wall card isn't built, but its state already exists in `huntGuide`, `refineGuide` and `Circulation.enoughQi`.

**Metrics:** the lessons are the D2-28 teaching spine; 7 silent hours is where "what do I do?" churn lands. The rewards (20-50 stones) don't inflate. No lesson is instrumented.

## Recommendations (ranked)
1. **Wall card as the free-play objective:** one verb per state with an icon, plus progress: Hunt (can't fund a run), Refine (raw qi waiting), Break through (bar full), Store (haul at risk). It reuses the guide logic and is the guidance players see for hours.
2. **One idea per chapter, all of them live systems:**
   - QC4: Home only. Seclusion moves to QC5 or the second session, at 30 s, ending on "Your peak works while you're away."
   - QC5: Region + Rumours ("The Borderlands are open; old manuals hide off its paths"). Board stays in Talk.
   - QC7: Build ("Place one piece on your peak").
   - QC9: the heart demon replaces Wall.
   - QR1: WeaponRack; QR2-3: DaoPractice.
3. **Teach the foundation grade on a card:** the QC4→5 breakthrough card, with one line ("New beasts and herbs deepen your foundation"). Never show "Unsteady" in QC.
4. Rewrite every line: elder at most 12 words, objective at most 5. Bottleneck: "Your art is stuck. Sit and spend stones to break it."
5. Funnel each lesson's shown and claimed events.
6. One lesson per QR third (QR4: garden aura and build items against the slowdown). Write the FE lessons as their systems ship.
