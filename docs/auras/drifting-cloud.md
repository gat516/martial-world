# Drifting Cloud Scripture aura

Covers the Drifting Cloud Scripture (Wind, rare) method aura (template `Effects.MethodAura.DriftingCloud`): its brief, what is built, its budget and gaps; read it when changing this aura, after the shared rules in [auras](../systems/auras.md).

## Brief
- **Idea:** qi drifting in on the wind
- **Mortal aura (the brief):** a soft cloud you sit on; pale wind streaks spiral in toward you on each inhale and loosen on the exhale; robes and hair lift faintly
- **Higher grades:** a slow vortex column around you; Heaven: a sea of clouds spreading out from your seat
- **Refs:** (none yet) (see [auras](../systems/auras.md#reference-images))

## What's built (Mortal; reworked: 13 loop emitters, no parts drawn)
- **On the body:** swirl strokes turning flat round the waist and the chest, shrinking inward as they turn (the wind spiralling in, thick on the inhale, loose on the exhale); wind curls drawn in from about a body-width to the waist; rising wavy wisps off the arms, torso and head and small curls blowing off the arms and head (robes and hair can't lift); a calm mint glow with a soft dot at the dantian; motes drifting on the wind
- **Ground / around:** the cloud you sit on: curling flipbook cloud puffs over a grey-teal underside, low mist drifting off it, a cool shadow under it (dark base); a soft wind vortex turning one way and a three-armed wind seal (the one SurfaceGui) the other, the seal tightening on the inhale
- **Breath beat:** at the start of each inhale: spiral ring strokes sweep in to the body, a gust of curls drawn in from all round, a puff of cloud at the seat
- **Ignition:** a soft flash and a swirl at the chest, the cloud bursting out in a puff ring (over a dark copy) with puffs thrown low, a gust swirl spreading over the ground, wisps and curls thrown up, motes scattered; limb wisps and curls surge

## Budget (R6)
13 loop emitters, about 50 live particles (the cloud puffs and limb wisps are the bulk), no light, one SurfaceGui (the wind seal), no parts drawn; its ignition adds ~50 particles for about a second.

## Known gaps
- Drifting Cloud: previewed in Edit mode on an R6 rig (grass at noon and
  night) through the plain emitter fallback; play speed, R15, phones and the
  live sit / switch / breakthrough ignition are untested. The swirl strokes
  lie flat, so they read best from above (the usual camera) and thin from
  level. Built without a reference (none in the library for a seated wind
  aura).
