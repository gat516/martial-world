# Stone Spirit, user's prompt (2026-10-23)

Create a stylized 3D cosmetic accessory set for a Roblox cultivation fantasy game for a Stone Spirit race.

Design only the accessories, not a full character.

Included cosmetics:
- Floating rock shards circling the shoulders.
- The shards should vary slightly in size and shape, with a balanced orbit-like arrangement.
- Stone plates attached to the forearms or shoulders.
- The stone surfaces should feature glowing jade veins running through cracks or engraved channels.

Style direction:
- Ancient, grounded, spiritual, and elemental.
- The design should feel like a living mountain spirit or jade-infused earth cultivator.
- Strong, heavy shapes should be balanced with mystical glowing detail.

Color/material direction:
- Stone: grey, slate, muted brown, or dark rock tones.
- Jade veins: luminous green or blue-green.
- Contrast the heavy matte stone with glowing spiritual energy.

Modeling requirements:
- Low-poly, Roblox-optimized, and cleanly separated pieces.
- Floating rock shards should be separate mesh objects, positioned in a ring or arc around the shoulders.
- Forearm or shoulder stone plates should be distinct accessories.
- The jade veins should be easy to recolor or swap later.
- Keep the design readable and avoid too many tiny floating fragments.
