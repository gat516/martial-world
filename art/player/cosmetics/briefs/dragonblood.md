# Dragonblood, user's prompt (2026-10-23)

Create a stylized 3D cosmetic accessory set for a Roblox cultivation fantasy game for a Dragonblood race.

Design only the accessories, not a full character.

Included cosmetics:
- A pair of swept-back antler-like dragon horns.
- The horns should curve backward elegantly, combining the silhouette of eastern dragon antlers with a noble, spiritual fantasy style.
- A short scaled tail.
- The tail should be compact, sturdy, and slightly draconic, ending in a small tuft or plume at the tip.

Style direction:
- Noble, ancient, powerful, and spiritually elevated.
- The design should feel regal rather than savage.
- Emphasize a celestial or bloodline-awakened dragon aesthetic.

Color/material direction:
- Horns can use dark bone, jade, pale horn, or weathered gold tones.
- Tail scales can be deep green, black, red, blue, or gold-accented depending on lineage.
- The tip tuft can contrast slightly with the tail scales.

Modeling requirements:
- Low-poly and suitable for Roblox.
- Horns should be separate left and right mesh objects.
- Tail should be a separate mesh object attached at the lower back.
- Keep scale detail stylized and clean rather than hyper-detailed.
- Preserve a strong silhouette and avoid oversized horns that overwhelm the head.
