# Beast Folk (Serpent), user's prompt (2026-10-23)

Create a stylized 3D cosmetic accessory set for a Roblox cultivation fantasy game for a Beast Folk (Serpent) race.

Design only the accessories, not a full character.

Included cosmetics:
- Scale patches at the temples and cheekbones.
- The scale patches should feel organic and slightly ceremonial, following the facial structure.
- A pair of small swept-back fin crests attached near the sides/back of the head.
- The fin crests should feel sleek and elegant, like a mystical serpent or river spirit rather than a fish.

Style direction:
- Mysterious, cold, refined, and slightly dangerous.
- The design should feel subtle and elegant, not bulky or monstrous.
- Focus on sleek contours and a spiritual serpent aesthetic.

Color/material direction:
- Scale patches can use dark green, teal, black, jade, or iridescent tones.
- Fin crests may have translucent or glossy material treatment if supported.
- Keep the overall palette cohesive and slightly mystical.

Modeling requirements:
- Low-poly and Roblox-ready.
- Scale patches should be modeled or cleanly represented as attached accessory plates hugging the face.
- Fin crests should be separate mesh objects.
- Keep the silhouette readable and avoid excessive complexity.
- No full snake body or exaggerated monster traits; only the listed cosmetics.
