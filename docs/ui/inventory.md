# Inventory windows

Covers the Inventory window (Weapons, Arts and Pouch tabs), the slots every item
sits in (Inventory cells and the hotbar), the rarity ladder, NEW badges, dragging
weapons and arts to the hotbar, the class glyphs, the bottleneck and Comprehend
controls, and sort, filter and search; read it when working on `Inventory`,
`InventoryFilter`, `Rarity`, `Fresh`, `SlotArt`, `WeaponView`, `IconRects` or
`HotbarView`. General HUD rules are in [ui](../systems/ui.md).

Rebuilt 2026-10-23 after the user's review ("I don't know which weapon is for which",
"so hard to tell what's common, what's rare", "I can't easily find a weapon or the
arts I want", "I don't need equip buttons if I can just drag it down"). Reworked
the same day after a playtest: "the golden frame on every item is very very tacky",
"i dont know about having weapons & arts in the same spot. do 3 tabs", "it looks
really basic because we're only using icons... using a photo of the tool", "not the
icons; the padding", and the toast "really conflicts with inventory" on a phone.

## Slots (`SlotArt`, cells, preview, hotbar, weapon slot)

- No ornament on things that repeat. A slot is a plain ink well (`slot_plain.png`,
  9-slice Rect(24, 24, 104, 104)) with its rarity in colour only: a thin `Edge` ring
  and a faint `Wash` rising from the bottom (`slot_edge.png`, `slot_wash.png`, white,
  tinted with the tier colour; ungraded things and empty slots get a dim neutral
  edge). All three are simple shapes drawn by `art/ui/draw_ui.py plain`.
- Everything inside sits in the slot's `Well` box (authored per slot, centred on the
  slot's hole): `Icon` (items), `Disc` + `Icon` (arts), `Model` (weapons), `Glyph`
  (items with no icon, 80 % of the well).
- Icons are cropped to their drawn area (`SlotArt.setIcon`: `ImageRectOffset`/
  `ImageRectSize` from `IconRects.luau`, generated by `tools/icon_rects.py`; rerun it
  after adding or redrawing an icon) and fitted, so each one centres with even padding
  whatever margin its source image has. Same approach as the seated Root tile. The rect
  keeps a clear margin of 6 % of the art each side where the source has room (`PAD`),
  so a shrunk icon's tips aren't cut flat by the quad's edge; SlotArt's fill shares
  are 1.12x so the art keeps its size. Icons drawn right to their border (IronJian,
  Fist, spirit stone) have no room: they need a margined redraw or sized copy.
- Arts: the glyph (tinted toward white) on a seal disc in the art's colour
  (`art_disc.png`, grey so one tint gives a dark face and a lighter rim, lit from the
  upper left). Cells draw the disc at 85 % of the well so the corner text stays clear.
- Weapons: the real model in a `ViewportFrame` (`WeaponView`), laid diagonally, hilt
  lower left, flat side to the camera, lit from the upper left, framed with a 4 %
  margin. **Live viewports, not thumbnails**: only weapons get one (a player owns a
  handful, plus the preview and the weapon slot), the models are static 9-24 part
  builds, and a ViewportFrame only redraws when its contents change, so a dozen cost
  little on phones; in return a new or retextured weapon shows with no render-and-
  upload step. The client reads `ReplicatedStorage.WeaponModels` (the same
  `assets/WeaponModels.rbxm` as `ServerStorage.WeaponModels`, mapped twice). Bare
  Fists (no model) show the Fist class glyph.
- Herbs and pills (wired 2026-10-24: Inventory cells, the detail preview, hotbar
  slots and the drag ghost): the same live viewport, the slot's `Well.Model`,
  through `ModelView` (src/client/ModelView.luau, also the Herbarium's cards):
  `SlotArt.paint(slot, id, count, countText, true)` calls `ModelView.item(frame, itemId)`
  for the Herb and Pill kinds (cores and treasures keep icons), upright, at 0.6
  transparency with none held, in ModelView's `well` light (ambient 215,208,196, light
  255,244,222: the cream-paper light left thin dark herbs near black on the dark
  well; the critic still found Starfall Bamboo, a dark-blue model, weak at phone
  size); SlotArt keeps each viewport's authored light and puts
  it back before a weapon is drawn there. Cells are re-made on each render, so a state
  push while the Pouch is open re-clones its models (herbs 15-85 parts, items 4-10);
  unprofiled on a low-end phone. `ModelView.item`: `ReplicatedStorage.ItemModels`
  first (pills, cores, dew, jade, peach), then the herb models (`Herbs.template`:
  `HerbModels`, `HerbModelsExtra`, the tinted stand-in). It returns false (and hides
  the frame) when there's no model, so the cell keeps its icon. Pose `upright`
  (default; plants stand) or `diagonal` (the weapons' lie); re-calling with what the
  frame already shows does nothing; the camera refits when the frame resizes. API in
  [herbarium](herbarium.md) "Model display".
- Names: cells Builder Sans Bold 12, one fixed size, two lines then truncated.
- **Hotbar models** (user, 2026-10-24: "since we have actual model views for herbs
  and pills... instead of words we're showing their model view in the hotbar"): a
  herb or pill slot shows its model (above) in the slot's `Well.Model` (every
  `Slot1-9` and `SlotTemplate`, authored), no words; `SlotArt.words` words a slot only
  when its viewport shows no model. The `Well` is authored at 64 % of the slot,
  centred across and at 0.57 down (the viewport is 1.2x the well, so the model's box
  is 77 % of the slot), bottom-weighted so tall herbs clear the count row: at 52 px
  (PC) the box is 39.9 px with 6.0 px left and right, 9.7 top, 2.4 bottom; on a
  touch phone (35 px slots) about 27 px. Picked over a centred 84 % box, where flower
  tips ran into the count (critic). A state push redraws nothing (ModelView's `Shows`),
  at most nine static clones. Cores, treasures and items with no model keep words.
- **Hotbar words** (user, 2026-10-23: "it doesn't make sense to fit icon and words
  in; you can't fit either... words only makes the most sense here"): a hotbar art
  slot (and an item with no model) shows only its short name (`Hotbar.label`: the def's `short` in
  `Techniques`, `Items` or `Herbs`, else the name's first two words), one word per
  line, centred in the box under the key and count row (`NameLabel`, y 0.28-0.92 of
  the slot, 2 px in from each side, under the cooldown sweep's ZIndex). Oswald Bold
  (condensed), cream, near-black stroke at 0.6 transparency (a full one filled the
  letters in at 11 px), `TextScaled` capped 15 px on PC and 11 on touch (`Layout`):
  measured 15 px at 1920x1080 and 1366x768, 11 at 844x390, 10 at 667x375, the same
  in every slot. Rule for a new `short`: one or two words, each at most 27 px wide in
  Oswald Bold 11 (about 7 letters; "Moonflash" is 33, "Bamboo" 27). Chosen by the
  critic over Builder Sans Bold, which held 10 px at 844 only by running "Heaven",
  "Phoenix" and "Ginseng" onto the slot's ring and fell to 8 px at 667
 . The key number stays top-left (keyboards),
  the count top-right (inset to 0.88, 0.07); while an art cools the words fade to
  0.85 under the seconds; a dimmed art (weapon not in hand, short of qi) or an item
  with none left fades them to 0.55. The full name shows in `Hotbar.NameTip`
  (BuffTip's pill) above the slot while the mouse is over it, or while a finger holds
  it 0.5 s with the Inventory closed (that release doesn't cast; a quicker tap does).
  Arts stay words; herbs and pills show their model (above); the weapon slot and a
  dragged weapon show the weapon model.
- The chosen cell and the hotbar's held item wear the same plain ring (`slot_edge`
  tinted cream, 3 px outside). NEW is a small crimson chip (kit `chip.png` tinted).

## Layout (`StarterGui.Hud.Inventory`, 760x500 max, centred; touch: fills the space between Roblox's top bar and the hotbar)

  back here when it closes, [herbarium](herbarium.md)) and the **Search** box (100 px

  `Lv n` (cinnabar at a bottleneck) or `xN` bottom-right, the slot number bottom-left

## Clipping audit (2026-10-23, "some icons / images are clipping")

Every visible button in the Inventory and the Spirit Market was measured against its
nearest clipping parent (the Grid, Rail and Info scrolling frames, the catalog), at
rest and grown 5% (`Motion.buttons` hover), with sample sections, cells (NEW, seal,
ring), chips, tabs, set tabs and tiles; the window and stack are the same pixel size at
1920x1080 and 1366x768, and the phone windows only change heights, not these margins.
Found and fixed:
- Rail chips: the first and last were cut 1.0 px by the rail on hover (no side
  padding). The rail has 2 px each side now: 1.0 px clear when hovered.
- The detail pane's preview (a Motion button) was cut 1.8 px at the top on hover. The
  Info pane has 6 px left and top padding (ui_lint: the hover halo and key hint clear).
- Icons: `IconRects` cropped at alpha > 40, shaving icons' soft edges and cutting glows
  (the pills, cores, spirit stone) flat; now alpha > 8 (`tools/icon_rects.py`), and the
  display-sized class glyphs have rects too.
- The top-right tiles' icon crops (see [ui](../systems/ui.md)).
Cells clear the grid by 0.1 px on the left when hovered (no cut); the bottom row by
7.7 px; shop tiles and tabs had no cuts.

## Class glyphs and colours (`SlotArt.classGlyph`, `SlotArt.classColor`)

One mark per weapon class, white and tinted: the glyph marks section headers, rail
chips, the detail pane and Dao rows, and stands in for a weapon whose model is missing
(and for Bare Fists). Arts carry no badge (removed 2026-10-23, above).
Fist ivory, Sword azure, Saber red, Greatsword orange, Guandao teal, Spear rose,
Staff green; universal arts a neutral cream. Glyphs: `art/ui/glyphs/class_*.png`
(`art/ui/draw_class_glyphs.py`): the kit's white weapon cuts for Fist, Sword, Saber,
Spear and Staff, padded square; Greatsword and Guandao are white silhouettes of
their item icons until Codex draws them (codex-sheets "Class glyphs and art icons").
A class art on the hotbar dims while its class isn't in hand (`SlotArt.weaponMissing`).

## Weapons: drag, no buttons

- While the Inventory is open a **weapon slot** (`Stack.Hotbar.WeaponSlot`, slot
  size, captioned "Weapon", 2 gaps left of slot 1) shows the model in hand on its
  grade's edge. It hides with the window, so the fighting HUD doesn't grow.
- Drag a weapon cell (or Bare Fists) onto the weapon slot or any hotbar slot:
  equip. Drag the weapon from the weapon slot back onto the Inventory window: put it
  away. Touch fallback: choose
  the weapon, then tap the weapon slot (tapping it with the weapon in hand chosen
  puts it away) or any hotbar slot. With no weapon chosen, tapping the weapon slot
  shows the weapon in hand.
- Every route calls `WeaponAction` ("equip" / "unequip"); the server checks
  ownership and busy state as before. Choosing an art or item that isn't on the bar
  and tapping a hotbar slot places it there; otherwise tapping a slot shows it here.

## Rearranging (drag and drop, `HotbarView`, 2026-10-23: "add swapping stuff around")

- Pick up: mouse, press and move 10 px; touch, hold 0.3 s without moving (moving
  first scrolls the grid, and the grid stops scrolling while something is held). On
  touch a hotbar slot can only be picked up while the Inventory is open, so a slow tap
  in a fight still casts.
- The ghost is the slot at 1.12x its size, popping in from 0.85; on touch it rides
  0.65 of its size above the finger so the finger doesn't hide it. The slot it would
  land on wears the cream ring (the held-item halo, 3 px outside); a hotbar item over
  the Inventory window fades to half (it will come off the bar). The target springs
  (`Motion.tap`) on a drop.
- Drops: a hotbar slot onto another swaps them, empty ones included
  (`InventoryAction "swap"`); a cell onto a slot places it, and whatever was there goes
  back to the bag (or, if the cell's item was already on the bar, the two swap)
  (`"slot"`); a hotbar slot onto the Inventory window clears it (`"clear"`); weapons as
  above. Anywhere else, the same slot, or Escape: cancel, nothing changes. With the
  Inventory closed (PC) slots still swap, but nothing comes off the bar.
- The server validates each (ownership, slot range, the technique cap) and saves the
  bar in `PlayerData.hotbar` as before; no new remote action.
- Hit tests use `PlayerGui:GetGuiObjectsAtPosition` with the input's own position, so
  the GUI inset can't skew them; the ghost is placed relative to the Hud's origin.
- No reordering inside the bag: the grid has no manual order. It is sections (weapon
  class, art class, item kind) sorted by New, Grade or Alphabetical, saved per tab, so
  a hand order would be thrown away by the next sort or section change; the sort and
  the rail chips cover finding things.

## NEW badges (`Fresh.luau`)

Kept in the profile: `PlayerData.seen` (id -> os.time() first seen), sent in the
state snapshot. Anything the player holds that isn't in it is NEW. Hovering a cell,
choosing it, pressing it on touch, dragging it, or having it on the hotbar marks it
seen at once (client) and in a batch about a second later (`InventoryAction "seen"`,
server-validated: only ids the player holds). The server forgets ids the player no
longer holds, so finding one again is NEW again. Profile data rather than a client
setting because the profile already persists across sessions and devices, and a
client-only list would lose unseen drops on rejoin. Counts: each tab's crimson tag
and the Inventory tile's seal (`Buttons.InventoryButton.NewDot`) count NEW things
not on the hotbar. A v9 profile counts everything it owned as seen (migration 9).

## Sort, filter and search (`InventoryFilter.luau`)

- Sort button cycles **New** (NEW first, then most recently first seen), **Grade**
  (best tier first, arts by level next) and **Alphabetical**. Ties fall back to
  grade, then name. The weapon in hand stays first in its row.
- The rail's chip shows one section; All (or the chip again) shows all. Pouch chips
  are the kinds of item (their first item's icon).
- Sort and filter are saved per tab in the profile (`PlayerData.inventoryView`,
  `InventoryAction "view"`), the same persistence as NEW, so the window reopens as
  the player left it on any device. No saved choice: New and All. Profiles from
  before the three tabs saved one `Arsenal` view: Weapons and Arts both start from it
  (mapped on load; the server splits it on the first save; no schema step).
- Search matches name, class or kind, grade label ("spirit", "rare", "sword");
  it clears when the window opens.

## Arts at a bottleneck

A chosen art shows its tier, level and XP bar (a `DaoList` row cloned from
`RowTemplate`). At a bottleneck (XP full at level 3, 6 or 9) the bar turns cinnabar
and reads "Bottleneck", the cell's "Lv" turns the same colour, two cost rows
(the class's Dao, with its glyph, or "Weapon Dao" for an any-weapon art; and Spirit
Stones; "need · you have") show up, and the Secondary button becomes Comprehend
(jade). While it can't run it stays lacquer and dimmed and its caption is the reason
("Meditate to comprehend", "Need Sword Dao 4", "Need stones"); a tap toasts the
longer reason.

## Kept manuals

A manual found before its weapon Dao (`techniques.manuals` in the snapshot) shows in
Arts like an art, its corner reading "Dao 8" in warning orange. Its detail says it
will be learned once the Dao is deep enough, the Pairs line reads "Requires Saber
Dao 8. Yours is 0.", and the Dao row of that class (every class for an any-weapon
art) shows the progress. No actions: it can't go on the hotbar (drag and slot taps
are ignored) until the server learns it on the level-up. Comprehending belongs to the seated Practice row's
Technique tile; the button here still works while seated (`Bottleneck` holds the
shared logic). Success is the server's notify.

Technique icons are flat white glyphs tinted by the technique's colour; the class
weapon skills' are drawn in `art/ui/draw_techniques.py`. Twin Phoenix Cannon,
Dragon-Piercing Thrust and Great Sage Awakening read poorly at hotbar size; a Codex
request for a matching art-icon set is in codex-sheets.

## Spirit ring ([haul](../systems/haul.md))

- `Detail.Actions` holds three kit buttons that share the row equally (the list's
  `HorizontalFlex` Fill, each authored 40 px wide): Eat/Use (gold), Hold (lacquer) and
  **Ring** (jade, `button_jade_h64`), items only. Ring reads "Ring n/m" (stacks in the
  ring / `ringSlots`) and puts the stack in; on a ringed stack it reads "Take out".
  It fires `InventoryAction ("ring", id, true|false)`; refusals (a full ring) toast.
  Hidden on rare finds (rarity order >= `Pacing.Haul.SAFE_RARITY`, which the server
  refuses as already safe); their Pairs line says "A rare find: it never spills, ring
  or not." A ringed stack's Pairs line: "In your spirit ring (n/m): safe even in the
  wilds."
- Pouch cells in the ring wear a jade 戒 seal top-left (`RingMark`, `seal_mark_jade_h48`,
  the 佩 seal's sibling, mirrored to (10, 8)); the glyph is Heavy and near white so it
  holds at 22 px.
- With nothing chosen on the Pouch tab, the Status line reads "Spirit ring: n/m stacks
  kept safe."
