# Evergreen Root Canon aura

Covers the Evergreen Root Canon (Wood) method aura (template `Effects.MethodAura.EverGreen`): its brief, what is built, its budget and gaps; read it when changing this aura, after the shared rules in [auras](../systems/auras.md).

## Brief
- **Idea:** an old tree, patient growth
- **Mortal aura (the brief):** green meridian lines that grow like branches from the belly out along the arms, lighting slowly over a few breaths; thin roots spreading on the ground; a few slow leaves
- **Higher grades:** the branches leave the body and form a tree canopy over you; Heaven: a dryad spirit rising behind
- **Refs:** spirit_manifest_green (see [auras](../systems/auras.md#reference-images))

## What's built (Mortal; reworked: 15 loop emitters, 22 beams, no parts drawn)
- **On the body:** a seed of green qi at the belly (a crackling green glow that swells with the breath, a hard dot, a sparkle cluster, the one PointLight); a green glow behind the body on a dark moss copy of itself; leaves budding off the shoulders and back in two layers (dark under lit): the tree's first crown; glowing vine meridians (a twisting strand texture flowing outward) growing from the belly over ~2 breaths, trunk first, then shoulders and hips, elbows and knees, hands, with bud glints at the joints; a green qi glow on the torso and arms, small shoots sprouting on the arms, leaves curling off and drifting down
- **Ground / around:** a dark moss pool with a green glow on it, a circle of linked rings (growth rings) turning slowly inside a jade ring of flourishes turning the other way (the two SurfaceGuis); five glowing roots (flat beams) creeping out one after another over the first breaths; shoots springing up round the seat; jade fireflies hovering; a few leaves falling from just above the head
- **Breath beat:** the exhale: the seed pulses with a glint, sap motes rise, a thin ring breathes out over the ground, a few leaves skitter out low. Every 3 or 4 breaths (0.8 s jitter, 1.3x swell) a bloom: shoots spring up in a ring, a green ring bursts open, leaves whirl up, the seed flashes and sparkles; arm shoots and leaves surge
- **Ignition:** a jade flash, long star and sparkle burst at the seed, motes thrown out, vines lashing up out of the ground round the body, shoots erupting, a dark spore puff under a bright bursting ring and a shockwave, leaves whirling up; arm shoots and leaves surge

## Known gaps
- Evergreen Root: previewed in Edit mode on an R6 rig (grass at noon and
  night) through the plain emitter fallback; play speed, R15, phones and the
  live sit / switch / breakthrough ignition are untested. The leafy crown
  behind the shoulders is the Mortal hint of the Spirit canopy; keep it
  small. Flat ground particles under a dark base need ZOffset ~1 to draw
  over it (smaller offsets lose the sort). Built without a reference.
