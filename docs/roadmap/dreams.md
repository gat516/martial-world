# Roadmap: dreams (far past the MVP)

Part of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md). Ideas from the map-redo talk with the user
(2026-10-24), parked here on purpose. **The game is cultivation plus a spirit garden
(user, 2026-10-24: "the original goal was to do cultivation + grow a garden").** None
of this is planned work; nothing here starts until that core is proven in playtests,
and anything picked up moves into the main roadmap first.

## Factions and paths
- **For now everyone is orthodox** (user, 2026-10-24: stay in scope); the rest of this
  section is later.
- **Every player belongs to an NPC sect or faction.** Three in the long run: the
  orthodox sect (the default, what exists), the unorthodox rogues (a river market town
  with a guild board) and the demonic Blood Sect (a red-rock ravine).
- **The demon path is found, then chosen** (decided; [replayability](replayability.md)
  "Paths"): never offered before it's encountered, choosable directly after.
- **Enemy cultivators come before their homes.** Blood Sect raider camps and rogue
  bandit camps in the wilds (the Borderlands' Deserters' Camp fits); a camp friendly to
  one faction is hostile to another, so one build serves all three. Already on the
  roadmap: section 8 "Cultivator enemies".
- **Player-made sects:** very far future (decided). Founded at a high realm; their
  grounds grow from the founder's home.

## The world shape (agreed direction, not built)
- **One place** (decided): no separate places for homes or wilds. Teleporting between
  places every trip home is too much friction for banking the haul.
- **The 8 spirit peaks stay** around the orthodox sect (decided), reached by the
  arrays as today. Gardens stay on the peaks.
- **Rogue and demon homes are small outposts, not full maps** (three maps is too much
  work): about a quarter of the sect's size, one compound (board, trader, elder) and
  a short row of home pockets in a different shell (cliff coves, caves), built from
  the same kit as the sect and peaks. Homes are cloned only when a player of that
  faction is in the server, so a server never holds more than 8. Each outpost's gate
  opens onto the shared wilds. Built when those paths are.
- **A faction waystation** at each gate into the wilds, where the haul can be banked,
  if the ride home ever feels like too much.

## The sect, rebuilt as a real sect
- **Courts opened by rank:** the outer court (Mission Hall with the board, Martial
  Field with the trial ring, puppets and the Dao drill, Herb Hall with the Herbalist,
  markets and the shared sect garden, the array plaza, the Testing Stone); the inner
  court at Inner Disciple (Treasure Pavilion, Scripture Pavilion, an Alchemy Hall held
  empty until alchemy); the core peak at Core Disciple (the elders' hall).
- **Every building is a verb or a view, never filler.** No disciple quarters (homes
  are the peaks): houses only as distant rooftops and lanterns.
- **NPCs make it lived in:** disciples sparring, sweeping, studying; the same for the
  outposts' guards, traders and cultists.

## Onboarding as an entrance trial
Moved into scope (user, 2026-10-24): [roadmap](../roadmap.md) section 2. Here only the
far part: each faction's own skin of it (a Blood Sect blood-pit initiation, a guild
bounty test in the river town), and removing or skipping the tutorial (far future,
decided); the trial makes a skip read as "recommended straight in".

## Tribulation and peaks
- **The tribulation comes to you:** it strikes on your own peak, the clouds gathering
  where the whole server can watch, instead of teleporting you to the summit disc
  (user: the teleport "feels kinda tacky"). Later, an optional remote spot in the wilds
  for a high-realm breakthrough. Frees the summit for the elders or a lookout.
- **Spirit peak kinds:** by element (fire with volcanic rock and vents, water with
  falls and pools, wood with bamboo and moss, metal with crystal spires, earth with
  terraced stone) and by path (a sect jade peak, a rogue's hermitage, a demon's cave
  peak with a blood garden). A vein grade that scales cultivation would need a
  progression-designer pass.
