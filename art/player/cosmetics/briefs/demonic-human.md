# Demonic Human, user's prompt (2026-10-23)

Create a stylized 3D cosmetic accessory set for a Roblox cultivation fantasy game for a Demonic Human race.

Design only the accessories, not a full character.

Included cosmetics:
- A pair of curved dark horns.
- Horns should feel sinister and refined, curving upward or backward in a controlled, elegant shape.
- Include either:
  1. a thin spaded tail, or
  2. a dark qi wisp emerging from the lower back as an alternative cosmetic.
- If generating both, make them separate variant accessories.

Style direction:
- Tempting, dangerous, aristocratic, and infused with corrupt spiritual power.
- The design should feel demonic but still stylish and cultivation-themed, not cartoon devilish.
- Emphasize dark elegance and restrained menace.

Color/material direction:
- Horns: black, obsidian, deep crimson-black, or smoky dark brown.
- Tail: dark matte skin or energy-infused material.
- Qi wisp: smoky shadow, black flame, or dark purple energy effect.

Modeling requirements:
- Low-poly and Roblox-ready.
- Left horn and right horn should be separate mesh objects or a clean paired set.
- Tail or qi wisp should be a separate back accessory.
- Keep forms readable and not overly thin or fragile.
- Avoid grotesque body horror; keep it sleek, mystical, and wearable.
