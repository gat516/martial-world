# Beast Folk (Wolf), user's prompt (2026-10-23)

Create a stylized 3D cosmetic accessory set for a Roblox cultivation fantasy game for a Beast Folk (Wolf) race.

Design only the accessories, not a full character.

Included cosmetics:
- A pair of wolf ears: taller, narrower, and sharper than fox ears.
- The ears should be upright with a slightly alert posture.
- Outer fur should be grey with subtle tonal variation; inner ear fur can be lighter grey or muted cream.
- Add slight fur tufting around the base and edges using simple low-poly forms.
- A bushy wolf tail: thick with layered fur volume, hanging lower and straighter than a fox tail.
- The tail should feel heavy and natural, with a gentle downward curve rather than a dramatic upward arc.

Style direction:
- Wild but disciplined, like a martial hunter or mountain spirit.
- The silhouette should be strong and readable, not overly fluffy or realistic.
- Keep the shape elegant enough to fit a cultivation world aesthetic.

Modeling requirements:
- Low-poly and game-ready for Roblox.
- Left ear, right ear, and tail should be separate mesh objects.
- Maintain clean topology and smooth shading.
- Avoid excessive fur strand detail; use stylized fur masses instead.
- Make sure the ears sit naturally on the head and the tail attaches naturally to the lower back.
